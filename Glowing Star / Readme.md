This project was developed as a semester assignment for the **Design of Electrical Equipment** course at the Department of Materials and Technologies. The goal was to design and build a decorative lighting device — a glowing star whose LEDs alternate between red and green.

## 🔧 Technical Scope
### PCB Design
Throughout this project, I gained hands-on experience with **Autodesk EAGLE**, creating the complete circuit schematic and PCB layout. Specifically, I worked on:
* Integrating standard and active components (such as the LMV358M operational amplifier, BCP53 transistors, and a custom LED matrix)
* Proper circuit routing, component placement, and layout optimization for both functionality and manufacturability (DFM)

This gave me a solid understanding of the entire PCB development workflow — from initial schematic capture to generating final manufacturing files.

### 3D Enclosure Modelling
Another key part of the project was designing a custom 3D enclosure for the assembled PCB in **SolidWorks**, focusing on:
* Exact mechanical tolerances for the board and components
* Precise alignment of cutouts for the LEDs and ventilation slots
* Structural stability and ease of assembly

### Manufacturing & Cost Analysis
The project also covered production planning and cost estimation:
* Compiled a complete Bill of Materials (BOM) with component pricing
* Compared PCB manufacturing quotes between EU suppliers (e.g., *Printed*) and Asian manufacturers (*PCBWay*)
* Defined PCB fabrication specifications: FR4 substrate, 1.5 mm thickness, green solder mask

## 💡 Project Deliverables
* **Functional Prototype:** An LED star where color switching is driven by transistors controlled via an input signal.
* **Complete Documentation:** Circuit schematic, PCB layout, 3D enclosure model, engineering drawings, and a manufacturing cost analysis.

## ✅ Key Takeaways
This project provided valuable hands-on experience beyond standard classroom theory:
* **End-to-End PCB Design in EAGLE:** Designing a functional circuit board from scratch to production-ready files.
* **3D CAD Modelling in SolidWorks:** Creating a practical, well-fitted mechanical enclosure.
* **Production & Sourcing:** Selecting components, comparing suppliers, and evaluating manufacturing costs.
* **System Integration:** Combining electronics, mechanics, and technical documentation into a single cohesive product.

Overall, it was a highly rewarding project that demonstrated how real-world hardware development works — from initial concept to a finished prototype — while strengthening my independent problem-solving and engineering decision-making skills.
